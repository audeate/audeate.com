MIMISOL · PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 7, 2026

Audeate合同会社 ("Audeate", "we", "us", "our") develops and operates the Mimisol mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App interacts with, how it is used, and — most importantly — what we don't do with it.

The short version:
  • 🎤 Mimisol listens through your microphone only to analyze the pitch of your singing — and it does this entirely on your device. Your voice is never recorded to a file, never sent over the internet, never uploaded to a server, and never stored — not by us, not by anyone.
  • 🔓 The App does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information to use it.
  • 📡 The App's core ear-training features work fully offline — pitch listening, scoring, and feedback all happen locally on your device's processor.

Information the App Interacts With

Microphone audio

Mimisol uses your device's microphone during ear-training exercises to listen to what you sing and compare it against the target note. This audio:

  • is analyzed in real time, in memory, directly on your device;
  • is never written to a file, recording, or log;
  • is never transmitted to us or to any third party; and
  • is never stored — by the time the exercise ends, it's gone.

We have no access to, and do not collect, store, transcribe, or transmit, any audio captured by the App. There are no servers in the loop — the microphone feeds straight into the on-device pitch detector and nowhere else.

Language / display preference

The App remembers the display language you choose (e.g., English or Japanese) so it can show the interface in your preferred language the next time you open it. This setting is saved only in local storage on your own device and is never transmitted anywhere.

Information we do not collect

Mimisol does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, or any other personal information. The App does not include account creation, sign-in, social features, or cloud sync — there is simply nowhere for personal data to go.

Permissions the App Requests

PermissionWhy we need it
Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) To listen to your singing and give you real-time pitch feedback during exercises. As described above, this audio is analyzed entirely on your device and is never stored or transmitted.

Children's Privacy

Mimisol does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because microphone audio is processed entirely on-device and is never transmitted or stored, no personal information is collected regardless of the user's age.

Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share user data with third parties — because, beyond the locally stored language preference described above, we do not collect any user data to begin with.

Data Security

Because your voice is processed entirely on your device and never leaves it, there is no transmission of audio data to secure in the first place. Your locally stored language preference likewise never leaves your device.

If Mimisol's data practices ever change — for example, if we introduce optional features such as ads, in-app purchases, or crash reporting — we will update this page, revise the "Last updated" date above, and (where required) request any new permissions explicitly before they take effect.
